Engineering Discipline

About Me

Taking hardware concepts from first principles to validated physical prototypes.

I am an Electronics and Communication Engineering student at St. Joseph College of Engineering, Chennai, dedicated to embedded systems, PCB engineering, and hardware prototyping.

My engineering work centers on taking technical ideas through the full hardware lifecycle: from mathematical and circuit simulation in LTspice, schematic capture and PCB layout in KiCad, to bench breadboarding, SMD soldering, and writing bare-metal or RTOS firmware in Embedded C/C++.

Rather than building purely digital abstractions, I spend my time at the bench: verifying sensor timings with logic analyzers, calibrating analog signals from accelerometers and gas sensors, designing low-noise power rails, and deploying local Wi-Fi web dashboards on the ESP32. I am actively seeking opportunities in Embedded Systems, IoT, PCB Design, and Hardware Engineering.
